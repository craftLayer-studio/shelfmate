## Pricing benchmarks and implication

| Benchmark | Annual INR |
|---|---|
| myBillBook Silver | ₹399 |
| Vyapar mobile Silver | ₹699 |
| myBillBook Diamond | ~₹2,604 + GST |
| Swipe PRO | ₹2,999 |
| Dukaan Starter (aggregator) | ~₹2,500–3,588 + per-order fee |
| Marg Nano (pharmacy) | ~₹5,550 |
| Gofrugal Starter | ~₹8,999 |

Implication: a kirana base plan must sit at or under ~₹3,000/year (₹299/month) to compete with billing apps. Pro at ₹6,999/year is justified only by visible extra orders and recovered udhaar. Pharmacy Pro at ₹9,999/year sits between Marg Nano and Gofrugal, which pharmacies already pay.

## Trends 2024–2026

1. **Quick commerce expands into Tier 2.** Orders doubled in FY25 (CareEdge, above). Basket top-ups shift first; monthly stock-up and credit purchases stay longer with kiranas (estimate).
2. **ONDC is growing but still small for kiranas.** ONDC reports 7 lakh+ sellers onboarded ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=493092)). DigiDukaan, a B2B procurement initiative for kiranas, reports 21,000+ retailers and 450+ brands across 3 cities ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/how-ondc-plans-to-digitise-b2b-procurement-for-kirana-stores-through-digidukaan/)). Treat ONDC as an add-on channel, not the wedge.
3. **WhatsApp becomes the commerce rail.** Meta moved to per-message pricing on 1 July 2025; utility templates inside the service window became chargeable from 1 October 2026; Indian accounts must migrate to INR billing by 31 December 2026 ([Meta pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)).
4. **Kiranatech consolidation.** Khata players are pivoting to lending and payments for revenue (see OkCredit losses above), leaving room for an order-led product.
