# ShelfMate go-to-market and onboarding

## Strategy in one line

Win dense residential pin codes one at a time, face to face, by showing owners udhaar recovered and orders received in their first 2 weeks.

## Beachhead

| Choice | Detail |
|---|---|
| City | One Tier 1 or large Tier 2 city where Blinkit/Zepto/Instamart are live (for example Pune, Jaipur, Lucknow, Indore) |
| Micro-market | 2 pin codes with apartment complexes; 80–150 stores within walking distance (estimate) |
| Store filter | Already takes phone/WhatsApp orders; has a delivery boy or family member who delivers; keeps udhaar |
| Avoid at first | Stores under ₹1 lakh monthly sales; supermarkets with existing POS |

## Channels

| Channel | How | Expected CAC (estimate) | Phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founder and field reps, cluster walks | 12–15 store visits a day in one pin code; demo on owner's phone | ₹1,400 | Month 1+ |
| Distributor salesmen referral | FMCG distributor salesmen visit 30–50 stores on a daily beat (estimate); pay ₹300 per paid store they refer | ₹500 + rep follow-up | Month 3+ |
| Store-to-store referral | One free month for referrer and new store | ₹300 | Month 2+ |
| Trade associations | Local kirana/chemist associations; demo at monthly meetings | ₹800 | Month 3+ |
| Customer-side virality | "Order on WhatsApp from Sharma Stores, powered by ShelfMate" footer on catalog; shoppers who also run shops sign up | Near zero | Always |
| YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels in Hindi | "How Sharma ji got ₹40,000 udhaar back" case videos; lead to WhatsApp demo | ₹600–1,000 | Month 4+ |
| Pharmacy distributor (stockist) partnership | Stockists recommend ShelfMate Pharmacy Pro to retailers | ₹800 | Month 6+ |

Paid Google/Meta ads are not a primary channel at this ARPU.

## First 10 paying customers: playbook

1. **Week 0.** Print 30 setup kits. Load the starter SKU library for the city. Build a list of 120 stores in 2 pin codes from Google Maps and a walk-through.
2. **Week 1–2.** Visit 100 stores. Pitch for 3 minutes (script below). Set up 30 stores on the spot (catalog of top 100 items, standee placed, 5 khata customers added). Aim for 30 free-trial stores.
3. **Week 1–2, same visit.** Ask each owner to forward the catalog link to their existing WhatsApp customer group or broadcast list. This produces the first orders in days.
4. **Day 3 and 7.** Call or visit each trial store. Fix catalog gaps. Send the owner a screenshot: "12 orders, ₹6,400 udhaar reminders sent, ₹2,100 already paid."
5. **Day 14–21.** Close: "Continue at ₹2,999 for the year with setup kit free, or ₹299 monthly." Target 10 paid from 30 trials (33% in this hand-held cohort).
6. **Day 30.** Ask each paid owner for 2 referrals in the next lane. Record a 60-second video testimonial from the best one.

## Sales script (Hindi-English, 3 minutes)

1. **Open.** "Bhaiya, Blinkit aur Zepto se aapke customers kitna order kar rahe hain?" Let them talk.
2. **Problem.** "Aapke regular customers ko aapse order karna mushkil hai: call karo, list bolo, wait karo. App mein ek tap hai."
3. **Show.** Scan the demo QR on their phone. "Yeh aapki dukaan ka catalog hai. Customer select karta hai, order seedha aapke WhatsApp par. Koi commission nahi."
4. **Second hook.** "Aapka kitna udhaar bahar hai?" Wait. "ShelfMate har 15 din customer ko politely reminder bhejta hai, UPI link ke saath. Aapko maangna nahi padega."
5. **Close.** "Aaj 10 minute mein setup kar dete hain, 30 din free. Fayda na ho toh band kar dena."

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Khatabook free hai." | "Khata yahan bhi free hai. Paisa aap tab dete ho jab reminders se udhaar wapas aaye aur QR se orders aayein. 30 din free mein khud dekh lo." |
| "Customers already WhatsApp karte hain." | "Haan, aur aap list padh ke type karte ho, galti hoti hai. Yahan order saaf aata hai, stock ke hisaab se, aur bill apne aap banta hai." |
| "Blinkit sasta hai, kya fayda?" | "Har cheez pe nahi. Aapke paas udhaar, loose saman, 20 saal ka bharosa hai. Bas order karna aasaan bana do." |
| "Time nahi hai setup ka." | "Setup main abhi karta hoon. 2,000 items pehle se list mein hain, aap sirf tick karo." |
| "₹299 zyada hai." | "Ek mahine mein ek customer ka ₹300 udhaar wapas aaya toh paisa vasool. Dashboard pe dikhega kitna aaya." |
| "Customer ka data safe hai?" | "Data aapka hai. Customer ki permission se hi message jaata hai, STOP likhte hi band. Hum kisi ko bechte nahi." |
| Pharmacist: "Online dawai bechna risky hai." | "Prescription wali dawai by default catalog mein nahi dikhti. Aap verify karke hi bhejte ho, apne local customer ko." |

## Onboarding checklist (rep completes at first visit, 30–40 minutes)

- [ ] Store profile: name, address pin, hours, delivery radius, minimum order, delivery charge
- [ ] Owner UPI ID verified with ₹1 test payment
- [ ] GST mode: regular (tax invoice) / composition (bill of supply) / unregistered
- [ ] Store type: kirana / pharmacy (drug licence number recorded) / other
- [ ] Catalog: tick top 100+ items from starter library; set prices for loose items; hide Rx items (pharmacy)
- [ ] Standee placed at counter; sticker on shutter or door; photo taken for records
- [ ] Catalog link sent to owner's existing WhatsApp customer list/group (owner sends from own phone)
- [ ] 5+ khata customers added with balances; owner approves reminder text and schedule (1st and 15th)
- [ ] Staff login created for packer/son/daughter
- [ ] Owner taught 3 actions: accept order, mark delivered, add udhaar
- [ ] Consent notice shown in Hindi/English on catalog; owner told about STOP and deletion requests
- [ ] Day-3 follow-up call booked

## Activation and retention

| Moment | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | First test order placed by rep from customer view | Rep |
| Day 3 | Call: catalog gaps, first orders check | Rep |
| Day 7 | Visit: weekly digest explained; add 20 more khata customers | Rep |
| Weekly | Owner WhatsApp digest: orders, sales, udhaar sent and recovered, stock-outs | Automated |
| Day 21 | Conversion conversation with numbers from digest | Rep |
| Monthly | "Top 5 inactive customers" with a one-tap offer | Automated |
| Any red flag | No orders 7 days or no logins 5 days: call within 24 hours | Support |

Retention levers: recovered-udhaar counter on home screen, weekly digest, annual plan, loyalty points that customers hold with the store, and data (khata history) that is costly to move.

## Referral programme

- Owner: 1 free month for each referred store that pays.
- Distributor salesman: ₹300 per paid store after 2 paid months.
- Customer-side: catalog footer "Apni dukaan ke liye ShelfMate" link to a demo request form.
