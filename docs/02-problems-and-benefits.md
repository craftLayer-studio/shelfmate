# ShelfMate problems and benefits

Format: problem, then ShelfMate's solution, then the benefit and the metric it moves.

## Owner

| Problem | Solution | Benefit | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Regular households now order top-ups on quick-commerce apps; a retailer body links about 2 lakh kirana closures to q-comm (see docs/04) | QR standee and shutter sticker open the store catalog; order goes to owner's WhatsApp | Store becomes as easy to order from as an app, without commission | Retention, ARPU |
| WhatsApp orders arrive as messy text and voice notes; items get missed | Structured cart with quantities and substitutes; order inbox with status | Fewer wrong deliveries, faster packing | Retention |
| Udhaar is 25–40% of transactions at a typical store (OkCredit estimate, docs/04) and owners hesitate to ask for money | Automatic, polite reminders from the system with a UPI link | Cash comes back without awkward conversations | Activation, retention |
| Notebook khata gets lost or disputed | Digital ledger with shared statement link for each customer | Fewer disputes; trust | Retention |
| Stock-outs of fast movers on weekends; expired stock thrown away | Low-stock list each morning; near-expiry list with "push as offer" button | Higher sales, lower write-offs | ARPU (Pro) |
| Distributor orders placed on calls from memory | One-tap WhatsApp purchase order from low-stock list | Less time, fewer misses | Retention |
| GST bills on paper or a separate app | Bill and GST invoice in the same flow; bill of supply mode for composition dealers | One app instead of three | Activation |
| Owner does not know who stopped buying | Inactive-customer list and win-back offer | Recovered customers | Retention, ARPU |

## Pharmacist owner (additional)

| Problem | Solution | Benefit | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chronic-medicine customers drift to e-pharmacies | Refill reminder by WhatsApp a few days before the course ends | Repeat sales retained | Retention |
| Expiry losses on slow SKUs | Batch-level expiry tracking and return-to-distributor list | Lower write-offs | ARPU (Pharmacy Pro) |
| Schedule H1 register kept by hand | H1 sale entry with prescriber, patient, drug, quantity; printable register | Inspection readiness | Retention |

## Staff and delivery boy

| Problem | Solution | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Owner on the phone dictating orders | Staff view with "to pack" list | Owner free for counter |
| Delivery boy unsure what to collect | Delivery card: address, amount, COD/UPI/khata flag | Fewer cash mismatches |

## End customer

| Problem | Solution | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Calling the shop is slow; not sure what is in stock | Catalog with in-stock items, no app install | Order in 1 minute |
| Q-comm apps do not give credit | Pay by UPI, COD or add to khata (if allowed) | Flexibility kept |
| Does not know balance | Statement link and reminders | No surprise bills |
| Spam fear | Separate consent for offers; STOP to opt out | Control over messages |

## Benefits summary for the sales pitch

1. "Your customers order from you on WhatsApp in one tap. No commission."
2. "ShelfMate asks for your udhaar so you do not have to."
3. "You never run out of what sells, and you stop throwing away expired stock."
